Equity Research

Nineteen modules, from a first annual report to a defensible research view. Built for about five hours a week.

0 of 19 modules attempted

The goal is not to memorise a syllabus. It is to be able to open any company's annual report, in any sector, and form a view you can defend to someone who disagrees with you.

The order below is deliberate. It starts with financial statements rather than market structure, because the statements are the actual gap and the market plumbing is already familiar ground. Each module ends with a mock test of ten questions pitched at intermediate to proficient — applying the concept, not reciting it.

Two things to settle before you spend months on this

Both are answerable with a phone call and neither should be left until the end.

Your eligibility route. SEBI amended the Research Analyst regulations in 2025 and the qualification requirement appears to have loosened from a finance-specific degree. Sources disagree on exactly what is in force. With an engineering degree and IT experience rather than financial-services experience, this single question decides whether the NISM certification alone gets you registered or whether you need an additional qualification. Check it against the current SEBI master circular, not a blog.

Your existing distribution business. Running mutual fund distribution alongside a Research Analyst registration raises conflict-of-interest and disclosure questions. It is very unlikely to be a blocker, but you want the answer from RAASB early rather than discovering a restructuring requirement after you have paid the fees.

The exam, briefly

NISM Series XV is the mandatory certification. A revised version took effect on 20 January 2026. The paper is 100 marks — 80 standalone multiple-choice questions plus five case studies carrying four questions each — over 120 minutes, with 60% to pass. The certificate runs three years and then needs renewal.

Registration itself is a separate step, applied for through the RAASB portal, and now carries a deposit lien-marked to RAASB in place of the old net worth requirement, graded by how many clients you serve. Confirm every figure against the current circular before you budget for it.

Nothing in this course is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. The worked examples use a fictional company so that no figure here can be mistaken for a real one. Regulatory details change; verify them at source before you act on them.
